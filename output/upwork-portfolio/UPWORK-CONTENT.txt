AI STUDY TUTOR

Project title
AI Study Tutor | Flutter PDF Chat & Homework Helper

Your role
Flutter Developer at APPZYS Technologies

Project description
I helped develop AI Study Tutor, an APPZYS Flutter learning app that connects study material with AI explanations and revision tools. Users can chat with PDFs and documents, focus on selected pages, photograph homework questions, and explore step-by-step solutions through follow-up conversations. Summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and saved notes support revision. The app brings document learning and photo-based study help into one connected mobile experience. Published on Google Play.
